Rapid Thrills Rafting Co.

Our mission is to connect adventure seekers with the raw power and beauty of white water rivers while delivering safe, high-energy, and unforgettable outdoor experiences for all skill levels.

History

Founded in 1998 by a group of outdoor enthusiasts, Rapid Thrills Rafting Co. started with just two rafts and a passion for guided river adventures. Over the past two decades, we have grown into one of the region's premier white water outfits, guiding over 50,000 guests down world-class rapids safely.
