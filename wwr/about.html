Rapid Thrills Rafting Co.

Our mission is to connect adventure seekers with the raw power and natural beauty of whitewater rivers, providing safe, thrilling, and unforgettable river expeditions led by world-class certified guides.

History

Founded in 1998 by a close-knit crew of river runners, Rapid Thrills Rafting Co. began with just two wooden rafts and an unyielding passion for wild rivers. Over the past two decades, we have grown into one of the region's premier white water outfitters, safely guiding over 50,000 thrill-seekers down legendary Class III to V rapids.

Today, our team of Wilderness First Responder certified guides continues our legacy of safety, conservation, and river stewardship, ensuring every guest experiences the adventure of a lifetime.
