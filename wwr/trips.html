Our Rafting Adventures & Packages

From gentle scenic floats to extreme Class V drops, find the perfect river journey for your group.

Family Splash Float (Class I-II)

Duration: Half Day (3 Hours)

Description: Perfect for families with younger children or those seeking a relaxing, scenic experience. Enjoy gentle ripples, beautiful canyon wildlife viewing, and a quick swim break guided by our expert river crew.

Canyon Rush (Class III)

Duration: Full Day (6 Hours)

Description: Our most popular adventure! Tackle continuous waves, fast-moving chutes, and exciting roller-coaster rapids. Includes a delicious riverside picnic lunch prepared by your guides.

Extreme Whitewater Expedition (Class IV-V)

Duration: 2 Days / 1 Night

Description: For true thrill-seekers and adrenaline junkies. Navigate roaring, technical whitewater rapids, camp under the stars in a remote gorge, and experience the ultimate river challenge.
